Experience

  • Nov 2025 - present
    Machine Learning Research Engineer
    MI-6 Co., Ltd., Tokyo, Japan
    • Project owner for three concurrent R&D lines in optimization and AI for science.
    • Budget-aware Bayesian optimization for autonomous experimentation: a closed-loop harness in which every proposed experiment is priced, charged against a budget, and refused if unaffordable, so policy quality is always reported beside spend. Twelve proposal policies, three acquisition optimizers, and a one-command reproducibility check that re-derives every committed figure from committed data.
    • Search heuristics for computer-aided synthesis planning: a learned cost-to-go estimate reused to reorder candidate reactions by remaining distance to available starting materials, improving solve rate and reducing search effort at unchanged per-step compute. First-author conference abstract.
    • An installable, versioned software component publishing a data contract to a separate optimization engine, with determinism enforced as a test, module boundaries checked at process level, and continuous integration gating its own published result counts.
    • Methodology across all three: pre-registered experiment designs, paired statistical tests with bootstrap confidence intervals, a stated detection floor beside every comparison, and negative results reported with their mechanism.
