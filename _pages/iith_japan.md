## Vice President, IIT Hyderabad Alumni Association, Japan Chapter

I serve as Vice President of the Japan Chapter of the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad
Alumni Association, working as a core member of the Executive Committee.

The role is about building durable connections rather than running events. I am responsible for
institutional and community partnerships: maintaining the link between the institute and its alumni
working across Japan, and developing **academia-industry collaboration** between IIT Hyderabad and
organisations here. In practice that means identifying where research interests on one side meet
industrial problems on the other, and making the introduction that turns a shared interest into a
conversation.

It is work I find genuinely useful. Having moved between an Indian institute, a Japanese university
and Japanese industry myself, I know how much of a collaboration depends on someone having already
made the first connection.
